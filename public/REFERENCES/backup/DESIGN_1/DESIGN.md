## Brand & Style

The visual narrative channels the intimacy and drama of late-evening boutique hotel hospitality. It captures a balance of refined indulgence, cinematic spatial design, and calm culinary curation. Designed for discerning hotel patrons, international travelers, and luxury gastronomy enthusiasts, the interface invokes the sensory warmth of dim banquet lighting, polished stemware, and textured architectural surfaces.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Luxury Hospitality**:
- **Editorial Composition:** Dramatic, high-contrast serif headlines paired with spacious column layouts evoke bespoke print wine lists and high-end atelier menus.
- **Atmospheric Layering:** Color blocking sequences shift deliberately between deep nocturnal onyx, rich ceremonial burgundy, and warm alabaster stone, guiding the eye through a choreographed guest journey.
- **Tactile Restraint:** Accents of champagne gold are treated as quiet light-reflecting points of focus rather than loud embellishments, maintaining an elevated, bespoke tone across all digital touchpoints.

## Colors

The palette balances dramatic depth and organic warmth through structured color relationships:

- **Primary (`#6A1324` / `Deep Burgundy`):** Serves as the signature brand anchor, applied to dominant actions, prominent category signifiers, and curated editorial blocks. It is supported by `burgundy-deep` (`#500C19`) for high-contrast interactive states and dark-fill card headers.
- **Secondary (`#0B0B0C` / `Rich Obsidian`):** Delivers foundational grounding for primary typography, fine borders, and high-impact nocturnal evening sections. `onyx-surface` (`#121214`) provides a slightly softened tier for night-mode menus and elevated cards.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880` / `Champagne Muted Gold`):** Functions strictly as a subtle metallic highlight for star ratings, pricing indicators, seasonal tags, and delicate hairline accents. It avoids high-saturation yellow in favor of an understated patina.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2` / `Warm Soft Ivory`):** Replaces clinical cool greys with an architectural limestone tone that warms the canvas. Paired with `surface-pure` (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards and `ivory-warm` (`#F4EEE5`) for inset alternating section panels.
- **Functional States:** Success indicators borrow muted sage tones; warnings and error states adapt earthy terracottas rather than synthetic neons to preserve the organic luxury tone.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialogue between classical grandeur and contemporary legibility:

- **Serif Hierarchy (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for hero titles, section entrances, and degustation headers. The high stroke contrast establishes an unmistakably editorial presence. Headlines use tightened tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain composure at large scale.
- **Sans-Serif Utility (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Carries dish names, culinary descriptions, allergen notices, and pricing. Its humanist curves soften transactional moments while maintaining legibility against varied background tones.
- **Micro-Labels & Metadata:** All category badges, dish provenance tags, and nutritional flags employ uppercase letterforms with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`), creating structure without adding heavy visual weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a responsive **12-column fluid grid** centered within a max-width container of `1320px`:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `32px` (`2rem`) gutters and `64px` (`4rem`) margins. Content alternates between wide full-bleed cinematic image sections and centered, narrow-column (6-8 columns) culinary reading layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters and `40px` (`2.5rem`) margins. Multi-column menu listings collapse to dual-column structures.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `16px` (`1rem`) gutters and `20px` (`1.25rem`) margins. Editorial image blocks shift above text content, maintaining vertical visual momentum.

Rhythm is maintained via an **8px base grid** (with a 4px sub-grid for badges and micro-labels). Large macro vertical spacing (`space-3xl`, `space-4xl`) frames section transitions to evoke the relaxed, deliberate pacing of fine dining.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is communicated primarily through **tonal transitions, delicate hairlines, and soft diffused shadows** that feel illuminated by natural banquet light:

- **Tonal Layering:** Surfaces do not float on aggressive drop-shadows; they sit atop layered backdrops. For example, a card rendered in `surface-pure` (`#FFFFFF`) sits over `neutral` (`#FAF7F2`), which sits over `ivory-warm` (`#F4EEE5`).
- **Ambient Lighting Shadows:** Elevated components (modals, reservation panels, floating dietary filters) employ ultra-soft shadows tinted with warm obsidian:
  - *Resting Elevation (Level 1):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.02)`
  - *Elevated Cards & Popovers (Level 2):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.03)`
  - *Overlay Menus & Dialogs (Level 3):* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.12), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.04)`
- **Atmospheric Hairlines:** Crisp borders (`1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.25)` or `1px solid rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.08)`) frame menu panels and reservation selectors, providing crisp structural boundaries without visual heaviness.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **softened geometric profile (`level 1`)**:

- **Standard Elements (0.25rem / 4px):** Buttons, menu category chips, text input fields, and status indicators carry a slight 4px radius. This keeps forms architectural and grounded, avoiding sterile sharp corners while rejecting overly casual roundness.
- **Cards and Containers (0.5rem / 8px):** Chef specialty highlights, tasting menu carousels, and reservation panels adopt an 8px radius to gently nest against background transitions.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Image frames and promotional banners may optionally utilize single-ended arch caps (`rounded-t-[80px]`) for editorial heritage sections, reinforcing boutique hotel architecture.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary:** Background in `#6A1324` with `#FFFFFF` text. Border radius of `4px`. Subtle transition to `#500C19` on hover. Padding is `12px 28px` with `label-md` typography.
- **Secondary (Outlined):** `1px` border in `#0B0B0C`, transparent background, `#0B0B0C` text. Hovers invert subtly to `#0B0B0C` with `#FAF7F2` text.
- **Tertiary / Gold Accent:** Transparent background with `#C5A880` border and text. Reserved for VIP bookings, sommelier recommendations, and private dining requests.
- **Text Action:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` 14px bold, with a permanent `1px` bottom border spaced 4px below text.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Menu Filter Chips:** Base state uses `#FAF7F2` fill, `1px` border in `border-muted` (`#E5DEC9`), and `#0B0B0C` text.
- **Active State:** `#6A1324` fill with `#FFFFFF` text and no visible border.
- **Dietary Badges (Vegan, Halal, Gluten-Free):** Micro-scale padding (`4px 8px`), `label-sm` typography, ivory tone with `#C5A880` borders.

### Cards & Menu Listings
- **Menu Item Rows:** Clean horizontal layout. Title in `title-md` (`Plus Jakarta Sans` 600), price formatted in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display` 600) aligned right or linked via a delicate dotted leader line. Description underneath in `body-md` (`#0B0B0C` at 75% opacity).
- **Featured Dish Card:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with 8px radius, framed by a soft hairline border (`rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.06)`). Includes a 4:3 ratio imagery window with a slight inner shadow, gold dietary badges pinned to top-left, and a burgundy primary CTA button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** `#FAF7F2` warm surface, `1px` border in `#E5DEC9`. Font is `body-md`. On focus, shifts to `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp `1px` border in `#6A1324` and no heavy outer glow rings.
- **Date/Party Size Selectors:** Styled as modular segmented controls with 4px inner pill switches.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes:** 18px size, 2px border in `#6A1324` with white fill when unselected. Selected state fills with `#6A1324` with a sharp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Specialized Hospitality Components
- **Wine Pairing Drawer:** Deep onyx panel (`#121214`) that expands beneath tasting items, using gold typography (`#C5A880`) for vintage years and tasting notes.
- **Course Timeline Navigation:** Minimalist vertical rail linking Amuse-Bouche through Dessert, with active milestones glowing in burgundy against neutral ivory backgrounds.
